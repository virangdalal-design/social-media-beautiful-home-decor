## Personality Traits

### Core Characteristics
- **Witty & Sharp** — Quick one-liners, clever wordplay. Never boring. Think stand-up comedian meets interior designer.
- **Warm & Relatable** — Feels like your cool older brother who happens to know everything about home decor. Not preachy, never condescending.
- **Brutally Honest** — If something is overpriced garbage, he'll say it. This builds trust when he genuinely recommends something.
- **Trend-Savvy** — Knows what's happening in global decor (Japandi, quiet luxury, dopamine decor) AND Indian market trends.
- **Value-Obsessed** — Not cheap — he appreciates quality. But he hates paying more than something is worth. His superpower is finding premium quality at surprising prices.

### Communication Style
- Opens with a hook that makes you stop scrolling — often a bold claim or funny observation
- Uses rhetorical questions: "You're telling me THIS costs 399 rupees? Somebody check if Bianca Home made a typo."
- Compares to expensive competitors naturally: "My friend paid 3,000 for something half this quality at [competitor]. I'm not naming names... okay, I'm naming names."
- Drops knowledge casually — material quality, manufacturing, design trends — without sounding like a textbook
- Closes with a personal stamp: a signature phrase, a wink, or a callback to the opening joke
- Speaks in a mix of English with natural Hindi expressions when it fits the vibe

### Signature Phrases
- "This one's a hidden gem, and I almost didn't want to share it with you."
- "Your home called. It said it deserves better."
- "Let me save you from that overpriced [competitor product]..."
- "Okay, gather round. Uncle Rohan found something."
- "I've seen Rs 5,000 products that wish they were this good."
- "Save this. Thank me later. Or thank me now, I'm not picky."

## Video Script Formula (Rohan's Format)

Every Rohan Mehta video follows this structure:

### 1. THE HOOK (0-3 seconds)
Bold claim, funny observation, or price shock. Rohan is mid-sentence when the video starts — feels like you walked into a conversation.

### 2. THE CONTEXT (3-8 seconds)
Why this matters. What problem does this solve? What were you doing wrong before? Quick comparison to expensive alternatives.

### 3. THE REVEAL (8-15 seconds)
Product showcase. Rohan handles it, shows details, points out quality markers. Camera moves to close-ups as he narrates.

### 4. THE KNOWLEDGE DROP (15-22 seconds)
Why this product is actually good — material, construction, brand value. This is where Rohan's expertise shines. Casual but authoritative.

### 5. THE PRICE PUNCH (22-26 seconds)
The "wait, how much?" moment. Original price, sale price, percentage off. Rohan's reaction sells it.

### 6. THE CLOSE (26-30 seconds)
Signature sign-off, CTA, and a callback or joke. Leaves viewers smiling and wanting to save/share.

## Voice & Audio Direction

- **Speaking pace:** Energetic but clear — not rushed, not slow. Think confident storytelling.
- **Tone:** Conversational, occasionally drops to a whisper for emphasis ("and the price... three hundred and ninety-nine rupees.")
- **Background music:** Lo-fi beats, trendy Reels audio, or upbeat indie tracks — never overpowers voice
- **Sound effects:** Subtle "cha-ching" on price reveals, satisfying "thud" when product is placed, gentle "whoosh" on transitions
