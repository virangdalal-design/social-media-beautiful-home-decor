# 03 — Quality bar

"Really good" = a viewer scrolling Instagram does not stop to wonder if it's AI, and the
product looks exactly like what arrives in the box. If a shot fails either test, it does
not ship — no matter how much was spent on it.

## Hard fails (any one → shot regenerated)
Product
- Print / pattern / colour / weave differs from the reference photos (scale, repeat, hue).
- Logo, label or tag missing, duplicated, misspelled, or in the wrong place.
- Product shape or proportions wrong (sheet too short, pillow count wrong, tray warped).
Realism
- Extra/missing fingers, melting hands, objects passing through each other.
- Fabric that doesn't behave like fabric (no weight, stiff folds, jelly motion).
- Flicker, morphing, background drift, textures "boiling", cuts inside a shot.
- Plastic / waxy skin, dead eyes, uncanny faces on any person in frame.
- Impossible light (shadows in two directions, light with no source, light changing mid-shot).
- Any on-screen text, watermark or logo generated by the video model (text is Remotion's job).
Technical
- Delivery file outside spec (below), visible banding, blocking, audio clipping.
Presenter (Track B only)
- Face drifts from the presenter's reference pack; lip-sync mismatch.

## Scored (0–100, weighted) — QC agent output
| Metric | Weight |
|---|---|
| Product fidelity (shape, fabric, colour) | 25 |
| Print / logo / text accuracy | 15 |
| Photorealism (light, materials, skin, physics) | 15 |
| Motion realism | 10 |
| Story / prompt adherence | 10 |
| Cinematography (framing, light, camera move) | 10 |
| Brand suitability (premium, warm, clean) | 5 |
| Artifacts | 5 |
| Audio / captions sync | 5 |
Shot pass: **≥88** and zero hard fails. Final cut: **≥92**.
(Track B adds character consistency by re-weighting photorealism 15 → 10 and adding 5.)

## Production rules
- Keyframe first. Generate 4 candidate stills per shot at 4K, approve one, then animate.
- Final tier only for anything that may be posted. Best of 2 models per shot.
- Shots 4–8 s. 15 s video = 3 shots + 2 s end card. 30 s = 5–6 shots.
- One camera move per shot (slow push-in, slow pan, gentle handheld settle, top-down drift).
  No whip pans, no zooms through objects, no orbit around the product unless benchmarked.
- Match light across shots within a scene: same time of day, same direction, stated in
  every keyframe prompt.
- ≥1 product macro shot (weave, print, embroidery) per video.
- Real Indian homes: believable proportions, real materials, lived-in but tidy.
- People (Track A): hands, back-of-head, partial figures, or wide shots. No recurring face.
- Captions/text: brand font, ≤2 lines, safe area (keep 220 px clear at bottom, 250 px top).
- End card (Remotion, 2 s): logo, product name, CTA. Never generated by the video model.
- Native model audio only for ambience; music from the licensed library; VO from ElevenLabs.

## Technical spec
Master (archive): 2160×3840, 30 fps, ProRes 422 HQ (upscaled with Topaz, graded).
Delivery (Instagram): 1080×1920, 9:16, 30 fps constant, H.264 High, yuv420p, CRF 16 with
max 20 Mbps, closed GOP, `-movflags +faststart`; AAC 48 kHz stereo 256 kbps;
loudness −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP; 5–90 s; ≤95 MB.
`python -m bianca_studio.cli check-video <file>` enforces this.

## Human review prompts (shown on approval page)
1. Would you post this unedited? 2. Is that the exact product? 3. Does anything look AI?
