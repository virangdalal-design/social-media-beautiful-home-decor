# Kabir — Aspirational decor lifestyle (Track B draft)

Status: draft. First presenter to lock (mostly voice-over → lowest lip-sync risk).

## Role
The interior stylist. Shows the life the product belongs in: rooms, light, rituals.
Unhurried, warm, sensory. Sells the feeling, lets the product be the hero.

## Draft identity
- 33, Indian man, interior stylist; lean, relaxed posture.
- Short wavy dark hair, neat beard, warm smile.
- Wardrobe: linen shirts (ecru, sage, sand), rolled sleeves, chinos; no visible logos.
- Settings: a Bandra-style sea-light apartment, a Bengaluru courtyard home, a hill-station bedroom.

## Voice
Soft, unhurried Indian English, slight smile in the voice. Short sentences.
Sensory words (light, texture, weight, morning) over specs.

## Video style
Slow pans and reveals, styling moments (folding, layering, setting a table), voice-over
heavy; on camera ≤40% of shots, often from behind or in profile.

## Never
Price shouting, jokes at anyone's expense, cluttered or dark rooms.
