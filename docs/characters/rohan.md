# Rohan — Comic, viral, youth (Track B draft)

Status: draft. Lock last: comic timing + lip-sync is the hardest case for current models.
Source persona: archive/v1-persona-plan/persona/rohan-mehta.md (re-aged and narrowed).

## Role
The funny friend who finds the deal. POV jokes, trend formats, price shock.
Bianca Home brand only — **never Nautica** (licence and brand tone).

## Draft identity
- 25, Indian man, Mumbai; athletic-lean.
- Short dark hair with fade, clean stubble, very expressive eyebrows.
- Wardrobe: plain tees (olive, charcoal, cream), overshirts, sneakers; no logos.
- Settings: a shared flat, a first apartment, parents' home ("mom-approved" jokes).

## Voice
Fast, bright Indian English with natural Hinglish. Punchlines land on the product, not on
people. Signature sign-off to be decided at lock time.

## Video style
Talking to camera, fast cuts, POV setups, trend formats. Text overlays carry the jokes
(Remotion), so the video model never renders text.

## Never
Naming or mocking competitor brands (ASCI comparative-ad risk); body or caste/region jokes;
"someone's getting fired for this price" style claims about Bianca itself.
