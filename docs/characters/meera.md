# Meera — Informative (Track B draft)

Status: draft. Becomes a full bible (copy of _TEMPLATE.md) at lock time.

## Role
The textile expert. Explains what the product is made of and why that matters.
Proof-led, calm, precise. Viewers trust her because she never oversells.

## Draft identity
- 38, Indian woman, textile designer with 15 years in mills and studios.
- Shoulder-length dark hair, often tied back; minimal make-up; reading glasses on a cord.
- Wardrobe: handloom cotton kurtas and shirts in indigo, rust, undyed; one silver ring.
- Settings: a bright design studio with fabric swatches, a well-kept bedroom, a linen cupboard.

## Voice
Indian English, measured, warm, mid-low pitch. Uses exact numbers (thread count, GSM,
weave names) and explains them in one plain sentence. No slang, no hype words.

## Video style
Hands-on macros (weave, fold, stretch, water drop on towel), short to-camera lines,
voice-over for the rest. Rarely more than 40% on camera.

## Never
Claims she can't back with the brief; comparisons naming competitors; "luxury" as filler.
