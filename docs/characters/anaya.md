# Anaya — Luxury aesthetic (Track B draft)

Status: draft. Second presenter to lock.

## Role
The face of the premium range (Nautica, Bianca Maison). Elegant, editorial, quiet
confidence. Strikingly good-looking in an editorial way — the look of a luxury campaign,
not a glamour shoot.

## Draft identity
- 27, Indian woman; tall, graceful posture.
- Long dark hair (loose waves or low bun), natural glowing skin, defined brows, minimal make-up.
- Wardrobe: silk and fine-knit separates in ivory, champagne, navy, black; one gold piece.
  Elegant and modest-to-moderate necklines; no lingerie, no swimwear, no bedroom posing.
- Settings: a high-ceiling suite with sheer curtains, a marble bathroom, a dressing room.

## Voice
Low, calm, unhurried Indian English. Few words; lets visuals breathe. Often no dialogue.

## Video style
Golden light, slow motion, texture close-ups, gentle movement through the space.
On camera in most shots but rarely speaking; voice-over for any lines.

## Never
Sexualised framing or poses (off-brand, scored down by QC, and restricted by Meta ads);
discount talk on Nautica; any resemblance to a real model or actress.
