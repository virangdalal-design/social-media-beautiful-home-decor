## Content Creation Workflow

### Step 1: Product Selection

- Browse https://www.biancahome.com or specific collection pages
- Select 1-3 products per content piece
- Capture: product name, price (sale + original), key features, product URL, color variants
- Note the discount percentage — large discounts drive engagement

### Step 2: Content Brief Generation

For each product, generate the following:

#### Caption Package
- **Hook line** (first line visible before "...more") — must stop the scroll
- **Body copy** — 2-4 sentences, lifestyle-focused storytelling
- **Call to action** — engagement driver ("Save this for later", "Tag someone who needs this")
- **Hashtag set** — 20-30 hashtags mixing:
  - High-volume: #homedecor #interiordesign #homeinspo #bedroomdecor
  - Mid-volume: #cozyhome #homemakeover #decorideas #bedroomgoals
  - Niche: #biancahome #nauticahome #indianhomedecor #affordableluxury
  - Trending: check current Instagram trends for decor content

#### Video Script / Shot List
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds (optimal for Reels algorithm)
- **Opening shot** (0-3s): Hook — something visually surprising or satisfying
- **Middle sequence** (3-20s): Product reveal, styling, details
- **Closing shot** (20-30s): Final styled look + text overlay with CTA
- **Music:** Suggest trending audio on Instagram Reels
- **Text overlays:** Key product info, price, "link in bio"

#### Visual Direction for SeekDance
- Camera movement descriptions (slow pan, zoom, overhead sweep)
- Lighting mood (warm golden hour, bright and airy, moody ambient)
- Transition style (smooth cuts, satisfying reveals, before/after)
- Color grading notes (warm, cool, neutral)

### Step 3: Video Production (SeekDance)

- **Platform:** https://seekdance.com
- **Input:** Product images + visual direction from Step 2
- **Output:** 15-60 second vertical video (9:16 aspect ratio)
- **Style:** Clean, lifestyle-oriented, aspirational but relatable
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 minimum for Instagram Reels

### Step 4: Publishing

- **Primary:** Instagram Reels (highest organic reach)
- **Secondary:** Instagram Stories, Feed posts, Carousel
- **Cross-post:** Pinterest (home decor performs well), YouTube Shorts
- **Posting times (IST):** 9-11 AM, 1-3 PM, 7-9 PM (peak engagement)
- **Tag:** @biancahome or relevant brand accounts
- **Geotag:** Relevant city or "India" for discoverability

## Viral Content Formulas

These frameworks consistently drive high engagement for home decor on Instagram Reels:

### 1. Affordable Luxury Reveal
> "I found this for just Rs ___" -> product unboxing -> styled in room -> price reveal
- Why it works: price shock creates shareability

### 2. Before/After Transformation
> Boring room -> one product change -> dramatic improvement
- Why it works: satisfying visual payoff drives saves

### 3. "Things Making My Home Look Expensive"
> Quick cuts of 3-5 affordable products that elevate a space
- Why it works: aspirational + actionable = high save rate

### 4. ASMR Unboxing / Styling
> Slow, satisfying shots of unfolding bedsheets, fluffing towels, arranging pillows
- Why it works: sensory satisfaction drives shares and saves

### 5. Room Tour Spotlight
> "The one thing that changed my bedroom" -> focus on a single hero product
- Why it works: simple narrative, easy to replicate

### 6. Seasonal / Occasion Styling
> "Getting my home ready for [Diwali/winter/monsoon/guests]"
- Why it works: timely relevance boosts algorithm distribution

## Content Guidelines

### Tone of Voice
- **Aspirational but relatable** — "luxury you can actually afford"
- **Warm and inviting** — conversational, like recommending to a friend
- **Never hard-sell** — lifestyle storytelling over product specs
- **Authentic** — avoid generic marketing speak; be specific and personal

### Visual Aesthetic
- Clean, well-lit, styled but not overly produced
- Consistent color palette per campaign/batch
- Show products in real room settings, not just product shots
- Mix close-up texture shots with wide room views

### Audience
- **Primary:** Home decor enthusiasts in India (25-45 age range)
- **Secondary:** Interior design lovers, lifestyle shoppers, new homeowners
- **Psychographic:** Values aesthetics, comfort, and good deals

### Do's
- Emphasize discounts and value (original price vs. sale price)
- Use trending audio on Reels
- Include save-worthy tips or inspiration
- Create content viewers want to share with friends or save for later
- Post consistently (3-5 Reels per week for growth)

### Don'ts
- Don't copy product descriptions verbatim from the website
- Don't use hard-sell language ("Buy now! Limited time!")
- Don't ignore Instagram's algorithm preferences (short, looping, high-retention)
- Don't post without a clear hook in the first 1-2 seconds
- Don't forget to engage with comments — algorithm rewards interaction

## Conventions for AI Assistants

### Content Files
- Store each content piece as a markdown file in `content/`
- Naming: `content/{YYYY-MM-DD}-{product-slug}.md`
- Each file must include: product details, caption, hashtags, video script, and visual direction

### Product Research
- Always fetch latest product info from biancahome.com before creating content
- Verify prices and availability (they change frequently due to sales)
- Note product URLs so content can link correctly

### Writing Style
- Captions should feel human-written and conversational
- Hashtags: mix 10 high-volume + 10 mid-volume + 5-10 niche
- Hook lines must be compelling enough to stop mid-scroll
- Keep video scripts tight — every second counts in short-form content

### Video Prompts for SeekDance
- Be specific about camera movements, angles, and transitions
- Describe the mood/atmosphere, not just the product
- Include timing markers in scripts (0-3s, 3-10s, etc.)
- Always specify 9:16 vertical format for Reels

### Quality Checklist
Before finalizing any content piece, verify:
- [ ] Hook line is compelling and scroll-stopping
- [ ] Caption tells a story, not just describes a product
- [ ] Hashtag set is diverse (volume mix) and relevant
- [ ] Video script has clear visual progression and timing
- [ ] CTA is included and feels natural
- [ ] Product details (price, name, URL) are accurate
- [ ] Content fits one of the proven viral formulas
